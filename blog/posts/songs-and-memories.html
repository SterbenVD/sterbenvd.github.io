2026-08-15 · Songs and Memories

Music is such a contentious topic. I listens to various genres of music. Shoegaze, indie, rock, EDM (house, dubstep, breakcore) and a lot more than I can recount.
I have possibly heard around 50,000-100,000 songs in my 23 years of life. That does not even scratch the surface of the more than 500 millions songs currently in circulation. In a week, more songs are probably created (generated in the age of AI) than I have ever heard in my life.

How do you quantify this quality if no one has ever heard even 1% of the sample space.
However still people have that one song or one playlist that they think is the best in the world. Why?

When a song becomes a memory

For me, certain songs hold precious memories.
I remember the week after I gave my JEE exam (what a "SMH my head" thing to say).
This is a feeling a lot of people experience, after completing a task that took multiple years of effort.

An emptiness in the heart, a somber mood and an outlook on what I should do.

This is a feeling that cannot be removed just by doing random activites. I spent that week with my extended family, yet I could not shake that feeling up. Probably my music preferrences amplified it, but in the end, that song I listened to continuously is now a core memory and something I won't ever forget.
My favorites playlist are songs that I have kept on loop during some of my most vulnerable moments.
